PSAURON assesses the quality of protein-coding gene annotation from CDS or protein FASTA sequences using an alignment-free machine learning model, flagging gene models that are unlikely to be protein-coding.

Reference:

M. J. Sommer, A. V. Zimin, S. L. Salzberg, PSAURON: a tool for assessing protein annotation across a broad range of species. NAR Genom. Bioinform. 7, lqae189 (2025). academic.oup.com/nargab/article/7/1/lqae189/7944703
